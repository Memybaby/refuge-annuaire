Heatstroke in Dogs: Signs and What to Do

Updated: September 2026

Heatstroke is a serious veterinary emergency that can be fatal within minutes.

☀️ Signs to recognize

  • Heavy, loud panting
  • Bright red or bluish gums
  • Excessive drooling
  • Unsteady gait, disorientation
  • Collapse

🚑 What to do immediately

  • Move the dog to shade
  • Wet them with lukewarm water (not ice-cold)
  • Offer a bit of water if conscious
  • Head to a veterinary clinic immediately
Never leave a dog alone in a car, even for a few minutes.

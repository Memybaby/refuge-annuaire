My Dog Ate Chocolate, What Do I Do?

Updated: September 2026

Chocolate contains theobromine, a substance dogs metabolize much more slowly than humans — that's what makes it toxic to them. The darker the chocolate, the higher the risk.

🍫 Signs to watch for

  • Vomiting and diarrhea
  • Restlessness or excessive panting
  • Increased heart rate
  • Tremors or seizures in severe cases
  • Excessive thirst

Symptoms can appear 6 to 12 hours after ingestion, and can last up to 3 days in severe cases.

What matters: the amount of chocolate, the type (darker = more dangerous), and your dog's weight.

📞 What to do right now

Don't wait for symptoms to appear. Call an emergency vet or clinic immediately. Don't induce vomiting without professional advice.

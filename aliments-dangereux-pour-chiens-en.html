Food safety

Dog-Toxic Foods: The Complete List

Updated: September 2026

Several everyday foods in our kitchens are toxic to dogs, sometimes even in small amounts. Here are the main ones to know.

Absolutely avoid

  • Chocolate — especially dark chocolate and cocoa powder
  • Grapes and raisins — can cause kidney failure, even in small amounts
  • Onions, garlic, leeks, shallots — damage red blood cells
  • Xylitol — a sweetener found in sugar-free gum, some peanut butters and candy; extremely dangerous
  • Avocado — contains persin, toxic to dogs
  • Alcohol — even in small amounts
  • Macadamia nuts — can cause weakness and tremors
  • Cooked bones — high risk of splintering and intestinal perforation
  • Raw bread dough — expands in the stomach and produces alcohol as it ferments
  • Caffeine — coffee, tea, some energy drinks

If your dog ate one of these

Note the approximate amount and the time of ingestion, then contact an emergency vet or clinic right away — even if your dog seems fine. Many of these toxins act with a delay.

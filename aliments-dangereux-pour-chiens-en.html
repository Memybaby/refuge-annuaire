Dog-Toxic Foods: The Complete List

Updated: September 2026

Several everyday foods in our kitchens are toxic to dogs, sometimes even in small amounts.

⚠️ Absolutely avoid

  • Chocolate — especially dark chocolate
  • Grapes and raisins
  • Onions, garlic, leeks
  • Xylitol — sweetener, extremely dangerous
  • Avocado
  • Alcohol
  • Macadamia nuts
  • Cooked bones
  • Raw bread dough
  • Caffeine

📞 If your dog ate one of these

Note the amount and time, then contact an emergency vet right away — even if your dog seems fine.

Understanding our 6 services

What we cover, and when to use it

Refuge Animalier lists six types of pet services, chosen to stay a reliable resource — from real emergencies to routine needs.

🚑

Emergency Vet

Clinics that accept emergencies outside regular hours, or 24/7. Use this if your pet is injured, poisoned, in respiratory distress, or in any condition that can't wait until tomorrow.

🛁

Mobile Groomer

Professionals who come to your home to groom your pet. Ideal for older pets, pets anxious about travel, or owners without easy transportation.

🏠

Emergency Boarding

Short-term housing for your pet when the unexpected happens — hospitalization, a sudden move, a family emergency. Booking ahead is recommended when the situation allows.

🩺

Veterinarian (regular appointment)

Clinics for routine care — vaccinations, annual checkups, spay/neuter, health follow-ups. For a real emergency, see Emergency Vet above instead.

✂️

Pet Grooming Salon

Grooming salons where you bring your pet to them. Ideal when a scheduled appointment works better than at-home service.

🐕

Dog Park

Fenced municipal spaces where your dog can run off-leash safely, following local rules.

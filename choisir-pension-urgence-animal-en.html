Making the right choice

How to Choose Emergency Pet Boarding

Updated: September 2026

Whether it's a sudden hospitalization, an unexpected move, or a family emergency, finding reliable boarding quickly can be stressful. Here's what to check.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Is the space suited to your pet's size and temperament?
  • Do dogs get regular outdoor time?
  • Is staff present at all times, or only during the day?
  • What proof of vaccination is required?
  • Is there a partner vet available in case of a health issue during the stay?
  • Will you get updates (photos, calls) during the stay?

Signs of a reliable boarding facility

  • Clean, well-kept premises during a visit
  • Staff who ask about your pet's habits and needs
  • Consistent, recent online reviews
  • Clear vaccination requirements — a good sign of seriousness, not an inconvenience
In a real emergency, spots fill up fast. Always have one or two backup options in mind before you need them.

How to Choose Emergency Pet Boarding

Updated: September 2026

Finding reliable boarding quickly can be stressful. Here's what to check.

🏠 Questions to ask before booking

  • Is the space suited to your pet?
  • Do dogs get regular outdoor time?
  • Is staff present at all times?
  • What vaccination proof is required?
  • Will you get updates during the stay?

✅ Signs of a reliable facility

  • Clean, well-kept premises
  • Staff who ask about your pet
  • Consistent, recent online reviews
In a real emergency, spots fill up fast. Always have a backup option.

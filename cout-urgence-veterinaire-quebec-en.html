How Much Does an Emergency Vet Visit Cost in Quebec?

Updated: September 2026

Emergency vet visits generally cost more than a routine appointment — here's why, and what to expect.

Why it costs more

24-hour emergency clinics keep staff and equipment available at all times, which raises their fixed costs. An "emergency fee" or "emergency consultation fee" is often added to the base cost, especially at night, on weekends and on holidays.

Typical price ranges

  • Basic emergency consultation: often between $100 and $200
  • Diagnostic tests (X-rays, bloodwork): can add $200 to $600+
  • Emergency hospitalization or surgery: can easily exceed $1,000 to $3,000 depending on severity

These figures vary a lot by clinic and region — always call to confirm fees before heading in, unless there's an immediate danger to life.

How to prepare

  • Consider pet insurance if your budget allows
  • Keep a small dedicated emergency fund if possible
  • Always ask for an estimate before non-life-threatening treatments

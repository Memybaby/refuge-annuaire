How to Calm an Anxious Dog at the Groomer

Updated: September 2026

Many dogs find grooming stressful — unfamiliar sounds, handling, the blow dryer.

🛁 Before the appointment

  • Get your dog used to being touched on the paws and ears young
  • Take a short walk before the appointment
  • Avoid feeding your dog right before

✂️ Options to consider

  • Mobile groomer: at-home service for very anxious dogs
  • Recurring appointments with the same groomer
  • Tell the groomer about your dog's specific triggers
If the anxiety is severe, talk to your vet.

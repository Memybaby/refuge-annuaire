How to Calm an Anxious Dog at the Groomer

Updated: September 2026

Many dogs find grooming stressful — unfamiliar sounds, handling, the blow dryer. Here's how to make the experience gentler.

Before the appointment

  • Get your dog used to being touched on the paws, ears and mouth from a young age
  • Take a short walk before the appointment to burn off some energy
  • Avoid feeding your dog right before, to limit stress-related nausea

Options to consider

  • Mobile groomer: for dogs very anxious about travel or around other animals, an at-home service can make all the difference
  • Recurring appointments with the same groomer: familiarity reduces anxiety over time
  • Tell the groomer ahead of time about your dog's specific triggers
If the anxiety is severe (intense trembling, aggression, total refusal), talk to your vet — there may be safe options to help reduce stress.

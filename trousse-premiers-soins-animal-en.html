How to Build a Pet First Aid Kit

Updated: September 2026

Having a first aid kit ready ahead of time can make a real difference while you're on your way to a vet.

The basics

  • Sterile gauze and pads
  • Medical adhesive tape (not sticky tape directly on fur)
  • Round-tipped scissors
  • Splinter or tick tweezers
  • Saline solution for rinsing a wound or eyes
  • Disposable gloves
  • A light blanket for transporting an injured pet
  • A soft muzzle (even a gentle dog may bite out of pain)

Information to keep on hand

  • Your regular vet's phone number
  • The number of a 24-hour emergency clinic in your area
  • Your pet's current weight (useful for dosing any treatment)
  • A list of their medical conditions or current medications
A first aid kit helps stabilize a situation while waiting for professional help — it never replaces a vet visit for a serious injury.

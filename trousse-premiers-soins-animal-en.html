How to Build a Pet First Aid Kit

Updated: September 2026

Having a first aid kit ready ahead of time can make a real difference while you're on your way to a vet.

🩹 The basics

  • Sterile gauze and pads
  • Medical adhesive tape
  • Round-tipped scissors
  • Splinter or tick tweezers
  • Saline solution
  • Disposable gloves
  • A light blanket for transport
  • A soft muzzle

📋 Information to keep on hand

  • Your regular vet's phone number
  • A 24-hour emergency clinic number
  • Your pet's current weight

My Dog Got Stung by a Bee or Wasp

Updated: September 2026

Most stings are harmless in dogs, but some reactions can become serious quickly — here's how to respond.

Normal reaction

Mild local swelling, slight pain, and sometimes persistent licking of the area. This usually resolves within a few hours.

Signs of a severe allergic reaction — emergency

  • Swelling of the face, muzzle or throat
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Sudden vomiting or diarrhea
  • Extreme weakness or collapse
  • Hives (bumps) all over the body

These signs can appear within minutes to a few hours after the sting. If you see them, head to an emergency clinic immediately.

What to do for a normal sting

  • Remove the stinger if visible (bee sting) by scraping it gently, without pinching
  • Apply a cold compress to the swollen area
  • Monitor your dog over the following hours

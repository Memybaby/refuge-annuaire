What to Do If Your Cat Stops Eating

Updated: September 2026

A cat that stops eating for more than 24 hours is a situation to take seriously.

🐱 Why it's concerning

A cat that doesn't eat risks developing hepatic lipidosis, a serious condition that can set in within days.

🔎 Possible causes

  • Dental problem or mouth pain
  • Stress (moving, a new pet)
  • Digestive issue or underlying illness
  • A poorly tolerated food change

📞 When to see a vet

  • 24 hours without eating: monitor closely
  • 48 hours without eating: contact a vet
  • With lethargy or vomiting: see a vet quickly

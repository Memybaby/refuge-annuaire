Recognizing an emergency

Signs You Need an Emergency Vet

Updated: September 2026

It isn't always easy to know if a situation calls for an emergency visit or can wait for a routine appointment. Here are the signs that should never wait.

True emergency signs — don't wait

  • Difficulty breathing or extreme panting with no physical exertion
  • Bleeding that won't stop
  • Inability to stand or walk
  • Repeated vomiting or diarrhea, especially with blood
  • A swollen, hard belly, especially in large breeds (risk of stomach torsion)
  • Seizures
  • Known ingestion of poison, medication or a toxic food
  • Trauma (fall, car accident, animal bite)
  • Inability to urinate, especially in male cats

Signs worth a call, but not necessarily a middle-of-the-night emergency

  • Loss of appetite for 24-48 hours
  • Mild limping with no apparent pain
  • Small behavior changes
When in doubt, call. Most emergency clinics triage over the phone — describe the situation and they'll tell you whether you need to come in right away.
